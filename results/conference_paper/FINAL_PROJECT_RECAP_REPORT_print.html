1. Executive Summary & Research Scope

This report presents the definitive scientific synthesis of the research project "High-Resolution YOLOv8-Based Plant Cell Detection and Architectural Ablation on Microscopic Sequences." The project investigated lightweight architectural modifications and geometric loss formulations for dense microscopic plant cell localization on high-resolution micrographs (3840 × 2160 pixels tiled to 640 × 640), operating under the strict hardware constraints of an entry-level edge workstation (NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3050 Laptop GPU, 4 GB VRAM).

Crucially, the study eliminates optimistic evaluation bias by enforcing a strict specimen-disjoint split across 16 biological groups, decoupling serial observations (0h and 24h). Through an isolated ablation suite of seven primary configurations (E1–E5b, E8), we prove that a 128-channel Bidirectional FusionNeck (E3) achieves Pareto-optimal efficiency (-32.9% parameters, -11.1% GFLOPs, 91.5 FPS, top F1 of 0.4915), while Shape-IoU (E5a) reaches peak detection accuracy (mAP50 = 0.6324) with zero inference overhead.

2. Problem Formulation & Single-Class Detection Task

Unlike natural scene datasets with extreme scale variance and sparse targets, optical plant cell microscopy is characterized by: (1) contiguous polygonal cell walls where instances share boundaries; (2) scale homogeneity; and (3) severe spatial degradation at large feature strides (stride 32 collapses micro-cells). Cell detection is strictly formulated as a single-class object localization problem (nc = 1, class_id = 0, class_name = "cell"). Ground truth polygon tracking indices ("1", "2", "34-1") represent lineage identifiers, not biological tissue classes. No synthetic labels (vessel, fiber, parenchyma) are evaluated.

3. Dataset Characteristics & Taxonomic Disclosure

The dataset contains 32 brightfield micrographs (3840 × 2160 pixels) across 16 physical specimen groups (plant_1 to plant_16) observed at 0h and 24h, containing 3,359 cell annotations.

Taxonomic Ambiguity Disclosure: The local repository README refers to specimens as "Arabidopsis plant lines," while cultivar designations "Sansha-5" and "WO115-2" are documented in literature as strains of the red seaweed Pyropia haitanensis. The paper transparently reports the specimens by strain identifiers and explicitly notes that formal taxonomic confirmation requires external curator verification.

4. Data Pipeline & Zero-Leakage Specimen Partitioning

Micrographs are tiled to 640 × 640 with step 480 px (25% overlap) and visible area fraction ≥ 0.30, yielding exactly 1,280 tiles. To prevent background memorization between serial 0h and 24h observations, splitting is enforced at the physical specimen level:

  • Training (11 Specimens): plant_1..5, 7, 9..13 → 22 micrographs, 880 tiles, 5,007 cell instances.
  • Validation (3 Specimens): plant_6, 14, 15 → 6 micrographs, 240 tiles, 1,502 cell instances.
  • Held-Out Test (2 Specimens): plant_8, 16 → 4 micrographs, 160 tiles, 1,247 cell instances.
  • Specimen Overlap: Strictly 0 (disjoint sets: Strain ∩ Sval = ∅, Strain ∩ Stest = ∅, Sval ∩ Stest = ∅).

6. Key Scientific Discoveries

  1. BiFPN FusionNeck (E3) is Pareto-Optimal: Slashes parameter count by 32.9% and GFLOPs by 11.1% while securing the highest F1 score (0.4915 at τ = 0.25) and boosting mAP50 by +0.1364 (0.6210 vs 0.4846). Standard 256ch PANet necks possess substantial feature redundancy for single-class cell detection.
  2. Shape-IoU (E5a) is the Accuracy Champion: Reaches highest mAP50 (0.6324) and mAP50-95 (0.5579) with zero additional inference parameters or FLOPs. Conversely, MPDIoU (E5b) suffers acute degradation (mAP50 = 0.4436) due to corner-distance instability on dense cellular meshes.
  3. Auxiliary BoundaryHead (E4) Regularization: Achieves 0.9038 precision with zero inference overhead, but compresses output confidence logits (τ = 0.25 recall drops to 0.0377). High mAP50 at τ = 0.001 (0.5422) demonstrates that boundary regularization requires lower operational thresholds (τ ≈ 0.05–0.10).
  4. Compound Diminishing Returns (E8): Stacking all modifications expands compute (+56% GFLOPs, 59.9 FPS) without exceeding lighter isolated models (E3 or E5a), illustrating multi-component gradient interference.

8. Hardware Efficiency on NVIDIA RTX 3050 Laptop GPU

CUDA event timing across 5 interleaved repeats (100 warm-up, 300 timed passes, NMS excluded) confirms real-time edge execution: E3 achieves 10.92 ms latency (91.5 FPS at Batch=1; 411.0 FPS at Batch=16 FP16). Peak runtime VRAM remains below 425 MB across all models (E3: 256.7 MB, E8: 422.0 MB), validating full feasibility on 4 GB GPU workstations.
